# devPassword - SPEC

One-page spec per devDesign PROJECT_TEMPLATE v3.0. The full design is `Personal_Password_Manager_Specification.docx` (v1.1). Where they disagree, this page and `docs/decisions/` win.

- **Class.** Personal. Only Nino uses it, it holds no data about other people yet, and nothing is reachable from the internet. Moves to Shared at Stage 4 (Debbie), or earlier if another person's documents are loaded into Nino's vault. See the challenge in `SPEC_REVIEW_2026-10-03.md`.
- **Problem.** Nino's credentials and personal records are split between LastPass (iPhone) and mSecure (Intel Mac app nearing end of support). He will not take on another subscription.
- **Success.** Nino's personal records live in one encrypted vault he owns: he finds and copies them on Mac and iPhone, sees what is expiring, and restores the vault on a replacement device from a backup plus his passphrase or recovery code. Live logins and passkeys are in Apple Passwords. devPassword holds a dated, read-only snapshot and history of them (decision 0007). Normal use never needs the Mac Studio running.
- **Riskiest assumption.** That a login snapshot can be refreshed often enough, and safely, without a plaintext file lying around. Tested by the credential exchange test in decision 0007. AutoFill is no longer in scope.
- **Version 1 scope.** In: record types secure note, payment card, bank account, identity document, the Stage 1 personal types, custom fields; read-only snapshot of Apple-mastered logins with change history and freshness; editable devPassword-mastered logins for sites Apple cannot fill; second secrets and "Pick characters"; links from records to logins; search; lock; CSV import; encrypted backup and restore; plaintext CSV export; iPhone read-only viewer over the encrypted backup. Out: editing Apple-mastered logins, password generator, AutoFill, passkey keys, one-time codes, attachments and scans, sharing, CloudKit sync (until read-only proves a limit), Windows, Android, AI.
- **People and data.** No data about other people in Stages 0 to 3. Before Debbie joins, or before another person's passport or card goes into Nino's vault, write the N6 note: purpose, basis, who can see it, retention of deleted records, deletion. Nobody is ranked (N7).
- **Architecture in brief.** Swift package `VaultCore` (model, crypto, SQLite store, backup, CSV) shared by a SwiftUI macOS app now and an iOS app later. One encrypted SQLite file on the Mac. The iPhone opens the latest encrypted backup read-only. Logins flow one way from Apple Passwords (decision 0007). Nothing exposed, no Mac server, no NAS, no tunnel.
- **Reuse check.** Done, decision 0007: Apple Passwords for logins and passkeys, devPassword for records and the complete record.
- **Failure and recovery.** Every change saved locally before it is acknowledged. A local snapshot (SQLite backup API) before every destructive change. Weekly encrypted backup to a chosen folder, newest four kept, each verified by reading it back. Off-site copy and restore cadence: see the backup section of ENGINEERING_STATE.md.
- **AI use.** None in the product. AI is used to write code: no real vault, CSV export, passphrase or recovery code ever goes into an AI session (N1).
- **Verification.** `scripts/smoke.sh` builds, runs every test, and runs the end-to-end smoke test on a 1,000-record synthetic vault. Manual checks on the Mac app are listed in ENGINEERING_STATE.md.
- **Deviations.** `docs/decisions/0001` to `0007`. 0007 supersedes the AutoFill and login-sync parts of the full spec docx.
